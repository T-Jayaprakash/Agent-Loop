Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

"Loopcraft" and "The Money Map" are private publishing tools used by one creator to upload and manage videos on their own YouTube channels. They are not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to them.

What the tools access

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tools use the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's channel and schedule them;
  • read the owner's own video list, titles and statistics, and update titles of the owner's own videos;
  • read the owner's own channel analytics (views, watch time, audience retention) to plan future videos.

What is stored, and where

The sign-in token and the analytics numbers are stored only on the owner's own computer and server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from YouTube viewers is collected.

Google API Services

The tools' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tools use YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it stops all uploads and analytics.
